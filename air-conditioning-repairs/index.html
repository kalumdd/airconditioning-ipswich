Common air conditioning faults we fix

  • Not cooling or heating properly: often a blocked filter or coil, a refrigerant leak, a failing fan or a sensor fault.
  • Water leaking from the indoor unit: usually a blocked drain, a split pipe or a unit that is not level.
  • Error codes and flashing lights: send us the code and the model, and we can usually tell you what it means before we visit.
  • Noise and vibration: loose panels, worn fan bearings, failing mounts or a struggling compressor.
  • Ice on the pipes or unit: low airflow or low refrigerant.
  • Bad smells: mould or bacteria on the coil and in the drain tray.
  • Remote, app or controller problems: from batteries and pairing to a failed PCB.

How a repair visit works

  1. Tell us the make, model, symptoms and any error code. Photos of the rating plates help.
  2. We give you the diagnostic visit price up front and book a time.
  3. The engineer finds the fault and explains the options and costs before doing any work.
  4. Common parts are carried on the van; others are ordered and fitted on a return visit.

Repair or replace?

We will tell you honestly when a repair is not worth it. Systems on R22 refrigerant cannot be topped up, and very old units can cost more to repair than to replace with a modern inverter system that uses far less electricity.

Repairs: common questions

Can you tell what is wrong from the error code?

Often, yes. Send the make, model and code and we can usually narrow it down before visiting.

Do you repair old systems?

Yes, but we will tell you if a repair is not worth it, for example on R22 systems.

What does a diagnostic visit cost?

We give you the price up front when you book. If you go ahead with the repair, we explain the full cost before starting.
