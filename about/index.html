Who we are

Local, careful and straightforward.

Air Conditioning Ipswich is the Ipswich team of Anglian Aircon, an East Anglian air conditioning and heat pump company. We are based at 30 Oulton Road in Ipswich and work across the town and East Suffolk.

We install, service and repair air conditioning and air-to-air heat pumps for homeowners, landlords and businesses. Most of our work comes from people who want a straight answer: what size of system, where it should go, what it will cost to buy and to run, and whether it is worth it.

Being part of a regional company means we can take on larger commercial projects and maintenance contracts too, while you still deal with a local team.

How we work

  • Free, written, itemised quotes with the make and model named
  • Systems sized for the room, not the biggest unit we can sell
  • Pipework kept short, straight and neatly trunked
  • Pressure tested, vacuumed and commissioned properly
  • A clear handover and a reminder when a service is due
  • Honest repair-or-replace advice

Equipment

We install and service equipment from established manufacturers such as Daikin, Mitsubishi Electric and Toshiba, and recommend the model that suits the room, noise expectations, controls and budget.
