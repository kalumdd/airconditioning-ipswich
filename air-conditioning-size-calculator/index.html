FAQs

Sizing questions

What size air conditioner do I need for a bedroom?

Most bedrooms need 2.0 or 2.5 kW. A loft bedroom or one with big south-facing windows may need 3.5 kW.

Is it bad to oversize air conditioning?

Yes. It cycles on and off, dries the air less, costs more to buy and can be noisier.

What is the difference between kW and BTU?

1 kW is about 3,412 BTU per hour. UK installers quote kW; portable units often use BTU.
