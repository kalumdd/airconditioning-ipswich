Ipswich air conditioning

Air conditioning in Ipswich, fitted properly.

East Anglia is the driest part of the UK and some of its sunniest, and Ipswich summers increasingly come with stretches of hot, still nights. Modern homes around the town are built to hold heat in, which is great in January and hard work in July. That is why more people in Ipswich are asking about air conditioning, and why most of them choose a system that heats as well as cools.

We are an Ipswich-based team at Oulton Road in IP3, so we are a short drive from anywhere in the town: Chantry and Pinewood in the south-west, Castle Hill and Whitton in the north, Rushmere and Kesgrave to the east, and the Waterfront in the middle. We fit wall-mounted and multi-split systems, service and repair most makes, and look after offices, shops and server rooms across the town.

Every job starts with the room: how big it is, which way it faces, how much glass it has and how you use it. From there we recommend the right size and type of system, give you a written quote with the make and model named, and fit it neatly with short, tidy pipework. No upselling to a bigger unit than you need.

Local knowledge

We know Ipswich buildings.

The right installation depends as much on the building as the room. A few things we see every week around Ipswich:

  • Victorian and Edwardian terraces around Christchurch Park, California and St Clement's: solid brick walls, narrow side passages and front elevations where an outdoor unit is best kept out of sight.
  • Waterfront and town-centre apartments: management company consent, balcony or roof positions for the outdoor unit and longer pipe runs.
  • Newer estates at Ravenswood, Pinewood and Grange Farm: airtight homes with big south-facing windows that overheat on summer afternoons.
  • Offices and units on Ransomes Europark, Whitehouse and around the town centre: cassettes, comms rooms and maintenance contracts.

FAQs

Air conditioning in Ipswich: your questions

More answers on the FAQs page.

How much does air conditioning cost in Ipswich?

A single-room wall-mounted system typically costs £2,000–£3,500 installed, and a 2–3 room multi-split £3,500–£6,000 or more. We give you a fixed, written price after a survey or a video. See our Ipswich price guide.

Can I get a quote without a home visit?

Yes. Send a two-minute video of the room and outside wall and we usually reply with a written quote within one working day.

Which parts of Ipswich do you cover?

All of Ipswich (IP1 to IP10) and the surrounding area, including Kesgrave, Martlesham, Woodbridge, Felixstowe, Stowmarket, Hadleigh, Needham Market and the villages in between.

How soon can you install?

Lead times are usually one to two weeks, and longer in a heatwave. A single-room installation takes one day.

Do I need planning permission in Ipswich?

Many home installations do not, but listed buildings, conservation areas (including much of the town centre), flats and units on the front of a house may need consent from Ipswich Borough Council. We flag this before any work starts.

Can air conditioning heat my home?

Yes. Almost every system we fit is a reverse-cycle heat pump, which heats rooms in winter for a fraction of the cost of electric heaters.

Do you service and repair systems you did not install?

Yes, most makes. Send the make, model and a photo of the rating plates when you book.

Which brands do you install?

Established manufacturers such as Daikin, Mitsubishi Electric and Toshiba. We recommend the model that suits the room, noise expectations and budget.
