Martlesham Heath was built from the 1970s on the site of the former RAF airfield, next to what is now Adastral Park, BT's research campus and home to dozens of technology companies. That mix makes for an interesting area: family homes on one side of the A12 and some of Suffolk's most equipment-heavy offices on the other.

Homes and technology businesses

At home, we mostly cool bedrooms and home offices, where people working from home spend long days next to computers and screens that add heat to the room.

For businesses, comms and equipment rooms need cooling that runs reliably all year, with alarms and backup. Offices with lots of screens and people need more cooling per square metre than an ordinary office, and we size systems accordingly.
