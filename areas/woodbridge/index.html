Woodbridge on the River Deben has one of the best-preserved historic centres in Suffolk, with many listed buildings and much of the town centre in a conservation area. Air conditioning here is about doing the job without changing how the building looks.

Air conditioning in period and listed buildings

For older houses we plan the outdoor unit out of sight from the street, often in a rear yard or behind a garden wall, and use floor consoles or slimline wall units where a large unit would look out of place. Listed buildings normally need listed building consent for any fixings or holes, and we provide the details the council needs.

Shops, cafés and offices on the Thoroughfare and around the Market Hill often have little outside space, so we look at roof positions and longer pipe routes before recommending a system.

FAQs

Air conditioning in Woodbridge: questions

Do I need consent for air conditioning in Woodbridge?

Listed buildings need listed building consent, and conservation areas may need planning permission for units on visible walls. We check before quoting.

Can the outdoor unit go away from the house?

Yes, it can be several metres away, for example behind a garden wall, with the pipework run underground or along a boundary.
