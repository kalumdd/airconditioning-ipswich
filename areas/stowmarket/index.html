Stowmarket has grown fast, with new estates such as Chilton Leys adding hundreds of modern, well-insulated homes. They are cheap to heat but can be hard to cool, especially in upstairs rooms facing south and west. Business units along the A14 corridor and shops in the town centre need comfort cooling too.

Newer homes and growing businesses

In newer homes we usually fit wall-mounted splits or a multi-split, with the outdoor unit at the side or rear. Some new-build estates have rules about external equipment for the first few years, so check with the developer if you are still within that period.

For shops, salons and offices in the town centre and units on the business parks, we fit systems that heat as well as cool, often replacing old gas heaters or portable units.
