Privacy notice

How we use the details and videos you send us.

Last updated

19 October 2026

Who we are

Air Conditioning Ipswich is part of Anglian Aircon. Contact us at info@anglianaircon.co.uk or 0330 520 2935 about anything in this notice.

What we collect

  • Details you send through our forms: name, phone or email, postcode and your message
  • Videos and photos you upload through the video e-survey
  • Basic technical data such as the page you came from, to see which pages bring enquiries

Why we use it

Only to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote and carry out any work you ask for. We do not sell your details or add you to marketing lists.

Where it is stored

Form messages are delivered to us by Formspree. Survey videos are stored privately on our own web hosting (Hostinger, UK/EU servers) and are not public.

How long we keep it

Survey videos are deleted automatically after six months. Enquiries are kept for as long as needed to deal with your request and any work that follows, and for customers for as long as we need to for warranty and legal records.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing us. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Cookies

This site does not set advertising cookies. Calculators remember small settings, such as closing a pop-up, in your own browser.
