Systems for commercial buildings

  • Ceiling cassettes for open-plan offices, shops and restaurants with suspended ceilings.
  • Ducted units hidden in ceiling voids, with only grilles on show.
  • Wall and floor units for meeting rooms, treatment rooms and smaller offices.
  • VRF/VRV systems for larger buildings, with individual zone control and heat recovery.
  • Server and comms room cooling, including duty/standby set-ups that run all year.

Working around your business

We plan installations around opening hours and occupancy, protect stock and furniture, work evenings or weekends where needed and keep you updated each day. You get a written proposal with equipment, layout, programme and price before anything is ordered.

Maintenance and compliance

  • Planned preventative maintenance (PPM) with scheduled visits and reports
  • F-Gas leak checks at the legal intervals, with records kept for you
  • Advice on TM44 energy inspections for buildings with more than 12 kW of air conditioning
  • Priority repairs for contract customers

Who we work with

Business owners, office and facilities managers, landlords and managing agents, schools and surgeries, and main contractors who need a reliable air conditioning subcontractor.
